# Design research → implementation

Primary-source review, 24 October 2026. This is a selected reference study, not an exhaustive ranking of current websites. Sources describe their own work; none endorsed this project. External sites were inspected as source documents, not comprehensively tested as interactive experiences.

| Reference | Observation | Transfer into this edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [eel Free School of Architecture](https://eelfreeschoolofarchitecture.com/) | The live source contains the pink `#e20a7f` and a broad collection of speculative architectural projects. | Keep the source link and pink lineage; give our independent experience its own authorship. Do not reproduce the school's image archive without a rights inventory. |

## Alternatives considered

1. Refined orthogonal towers: strong comparison baseline, limited spatial variation. Retained as Terraces.
2. Fully fluid animated organisms: visually expressive, but persistent motion and opaque mapping weaken comparison. Not implemented.
3. Contour pavilions: a common radial grammar, inspectable voids, slabs and ribs. Selected, with formulas in METHOD.md and the entity panel.

The composition moves long explanation into a disclosure and reading room. The initial desktop shows an editorial introduction, an unobstructed model center and a bottom frame switcher. The introduction clears after direct scene interaction. On mobile, controls use a bottom band and the detailed record becomes a sheet.

## Retrieval boundaries

The web reader could not retrieve eel directly; a separate anonymous public HTTP fetch returned its HTML successfully. Universal Everything's correct primary domain is `universaleverything.com`, without a hyphen. OMA and Active Theory exposed little meaningful content in the text reader; they were not treated as evaluated references. No external images, fonts, video or code were copied from these examples.
