One school,
surveyed three ways.

A speculative city built from a question: what if the reading of an architectural school were an instrument you could walk through, argue with, and change?

The experiment

Twenty-one entities of architectural education — schools, satellites, historical experiments and the regulatory baseline — are given built form by eight authored measures. Three frames ask three different questions of the same data, and the city rearranges itself in answer. Every score is published, every formula is documented, and every disagreement with them is welcome: the instrument exists so that the reading can be contested.

Who made it

Semir Poturak — architect, university educator and PhD researcher working on architectural education, computation and fabrication. The city was drawn, tested and verified inside Svemir, his open working universe in which several AI minds plan, build and check one another's work in parallel. This project is one public record of what that collaboration can produce.

Independence

Inspired by eel Free School of Architecture, whose published ideas set the questions. It is independently made: not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by the school. Scores and relationships are provisional interpretations by the author — a basis for conversation, not institutional ratings.

Collaboration

The entire instrument is open source under the MIT license. Correct a score, propose an entity, add a frame, or fork the whole city for another field of knowledge. Repository ↗ · Start a conversation ↗
