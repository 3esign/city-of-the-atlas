HOW TO READ THE CITY

Eight measures. Three questions.

Each measure is an authored score from 0 to 5. Equal totals share a rank. Heights use model units, not actual building dimensions; the baseline is 9 units and the maximum is 95. In contour mode, eight measures occupy fixed angular directions; access opens the atrium; evidence sets floor count; autonomy minus recognition sets twist. Terraces offer the previous sorted measure representation. See the complete mapping. Connecting lines are proposed analytical relationships.

F01: Autonomy + World + Matter + Episteme + Access.
F02: 2 × Recognition + 2 × Evidence + Matter.
F03: 3 × Cosmology + World + Matter.

These are our formulas. F02 does not determine registration eligibility; F03 is an interpretation inspired by cosmotechnics, not a measurement proposed by Yuk Hui.

Only the atlas prototype is implemented here. All other plots represent proposals, not an assessment that the school has no existing work.

Translate one of the published material ideas into a documented specimen: fabrication parameters, measurements, photographs and a comparison with a simpler baseline.

D · PROPOSAL

L · PROPOSAL

Turn the collected images into a browsable catalogue with authors, titles, dates, rights and source links. Confirm the inventory and permissions before publication; evaluate IIIF where it adds value.

COLOPHON · AN OPEN EXPERIMENT

Who made this, and on what terms.

The experiment

Twenty-one entities of architectural education given built form by eight authored measures and three frames. Every score is published, every formula documented, and every disagreement with them welcome: the instrument exists so that the reading can be contested.

Who made it

Semir Poturak — architect, university educator and PhD researcher working on architectural education, computation and fabrication. Drawn, tested and verified inside Svemir, his open working universe in which several AI minds plan, build and check one another's work in parallel.

Independence

Inspired by eel Free School of Architecture. Independently made; not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by the school. Scores are provisional interpretations, not institutional ratings.

Collaboration

Open source under the MIT license. Correct a score, propose an entity, add a frame, or fork the whole city for another field of knowledge. Repository ↗ · Start a conversation ↗
